## Why Packaging Compliance Matters

Food packaging isn't just about aesthetics — it's a legal requirement. Non-compliant packaging can result in product recalls, heavy fines, and damage to your brand reputation.

## Mandatory Label Declarations

Every packaged food product in India must display:

1. **Product Name** — Common or generic name
2. **List of Ingredients** — In descending order of composition
3. **Nutritional Information** — Per 100g/ml and per serving
4. **Net Quantity** — In metric units
5. **FSSAI License Number** — With the FSSAI logo
6. **Date of Manufacturing** — And best before/use by date
7. **Name & Address** — Of manufacturer/packer/importer
8. **Country of Origin** — For imported products
9. **Veg/Non-Veg Symbol** — Green dot or brown dot

## Recent FSSAI Updates (2025)

### Front-of-Pack Labelling
FSSAI has introduced front-of-pack nutritional labelling requirements:
- Health star rating system
- Warning labels for high sugar, salt, and fat

### Allergen Declarations
Enhanced allergen labelling now mandatory:
- Must be highlighted or in bold
- "Contains" and "May contain" statements required

## Packaging Material Requirements

- Must be food-grade certified
- BIS standards compliance
- Migration testing for plastic packaging
- Sustainable packaging guidelines

## Common Packaging Mistakes

1. Missing FSSAI logo or license number
2. Incorrect nutritional calculations
3. Wrong veg/non-veg symbol placement
4. Illegible font size (minimum 1.5mm for mandatory info)
5. Missing allergen declarations
